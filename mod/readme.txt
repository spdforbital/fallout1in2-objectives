Fallout 1in2 Objectives
=======================
- Quest panel on the right of the map screen (also on the world map): main quest first,
  then optional quests, with the exact next step, an arrow and the distance in hexes.
  Steps that can be done several ways show the other ways as "or: ...".
- Map markers: the people and objects your current steps point at get a purple outline;
  targets off screen get an arrow with the distance at the screen edge.
- Outlines: quest items yellow, weapons/armor better than yours green (no Motion Sensor).
- O opens the Objectives window: 1 Quests (every quest as a tree: locked / not started /
  active / done / failed, what unlocks it and who gives it; companions in their own
  section), 2 Gear (better gear on this map, nearest first), 3 Settings.
Settings: objectives.ini in this folder (or in game: O, then 3).
Uninstall: delete this folder and the "fo1_objectives" line in mods\mods_order.txt.
